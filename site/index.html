THE GAME PLAN

Good advice starts
with honest data.

What updates automatically?

NFL week, player teams, reported injury status, depth-chart information and 24-hour add/drop trends refresh through a scheduled GitHub Actions job every Tuesday. A timestamp shows the last successful download. Schedules can be delayed.

How are waiver notes generated?

Rules flag reported injuries and depth-chart roles. Add counts measure interest, not talent or guaranteed opportunity. Check your league's available players and official team reports before making a claim.

How does the scoring work?

Passing yards × 0.04 + passing TDs × 4 − interceptions × 2 + rushing and receiving yards × 0.1 + rushing and receiving TDs × 6 + catches × reception bonus. Inputs start at zero; enter your own expectations. Trade values are also supplied by you.
